GoaMitra Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

GoaMitra is a prototype Goa travel app. This page explains what information the app uses and why.

Information we use

  • Name and travel preferences: your name, travel type, travel month, group size and interests, which you enter when you start the app. These are saved only on your own device.
  • Location: if you allow it, the app reads your approximate location to suggest nearby places and give directions. It is stored only for your current session on your device.
  • Chat messages: the questions you type or speak to our AI assistant (GAI), together with your preferences and location, are sent to a third-party AI service (Google Gemini API) to generate answers.
  • Voice input: if you tap the microphone, your browser's speech recognition converts your speech to text. Audio handling is done by your device or browser provider.

How location is processed

To show your area name, your coordinates may be sent to a reverse-geocoding service (BigDataCloud) to look up the place name.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or password.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not show ads.

Data storage and deletion

Your name and preferences stay on your device. You can delete them any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. We do not keep a server-side copy of your profile.

Children

GoaMitra is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
